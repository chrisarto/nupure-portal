⚠️ Mandatory PPE & Chemical Handling Rules

  • Eye Protection (Safety Glasses): Mandatory when handling or decanting Blue Lazer (Corrosive to Eyes — H318) and recommended when decanting any chemical.
  • Hand & Foot Protection: Disposable nitrile rubber gloves and closed work/safety boots must be worn for all cleaning & hygiene tasks. Change gloves between zones.
  • Label & Dispensing Control: Only dispense at authorised points into correctly labelled trigger bottles/buckets. Never reuse food or drink containers.
  • Emergency / Poisons Info: Australia Poisons Information Centre: 13 11 26. Ensure eyewash stations are unobstructed.

🎨 Colour-Coded Equipment Segregation (MJH-SOP-NUPURE-001)

RED — Toilets & Urinals:
Toilet bowls, urinals, sanitary bins (SB-01..10) & cubicle floors. Never use in sinks, kitchens or offices.
YELLOW — Washrooms & Showers:
Basins, taps, mirrors, showers, vanity tops & tiled walls. Never use on toilets or food areas.
GREEN — Lunchrooms & Kitchens:
Dining tables, food benches, microwaves, fridges & sinks. Dedicated food-area cloths only.
BLUE — Offices & General Floors:
General offices, boardrooms, reception, internal glass & Laboratory vinyl floor (SOP-04).

⛔ Critical Nu-Pure Site Boundaries

  • NEVER Mix Chemicals: Never combine Blue Lazer (acid) with bleach/chlorine or alkaline cleaners — toxic gas hazard.
  • Strict Laboratory Rule (NP-PR-04 / SOP-04): Use Use All with clean BLUE floor tools on the vinyl floor ONLY. Do NOT touch lab benches, chemicals, samples or instruments.
  • Managed Air Fresheners (AF-01 to AF-09): Keep aerosols away from ignition sources; do not spray near people, food or open electricals.
  • 5-Year SDS Review Control: All site SDS documents are controlled in the Portal (/documents) and reviewed at least every 5 years from issue date.
